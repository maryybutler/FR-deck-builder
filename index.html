The world of Fractured Realms

Every realm has a story.

The Seven Crystals

Seven living crystal types emerged from the wounds of the Earth, each carrying a force capable of transforming reality. Their power reshaped civilisation, gave rise to rival factions and continues to define the struggle for the future.

Yellow · Life · Growth · Evolution

The Bloom Shards

Yellow spread through the Earth like roots, swallowing cities in forests and accelerating the evolution of living creatures. Its followers wield healing and regeneration, but risk losing their individuality to the Verdant Choir.

Gameplay theme: Healing, regeneration and biological growth.

Blue · Intellect · Logic · Memory

The Mind Shard

Blue awakened machines and granted humans extraordinary intelligence and predictive abilities. Its machine-states seek absolute order, even as emotion fades from those who bond with the crystal.

Gameplay theme: Calculation, prediction and intelligent machinery.

Red · Heat · Industry · Creation

The Industry Shard

Red fuels colossal engines, mechanised armies and walking fortresses. Its users reshape metal and wield intense heat, while its influence amplifies aggression and ambition.

Gameplay theme: Heat, industrial creation and powerful machinery.

Purple · Death · Spirits · The Vanish Realm

The Dimensional Shard

Purple opened tears into the Vanish Realm, a dark mirror filled with lost souls and ancient entities. Its users summon echoes of the dead and cross between worlds, risking possession by what lies beyond.

Gameplay theme: Spirits, death and dimensional manipulation.

Green · Adaptation · Mutation · Instinct

The Toxic Shard

Green transforms ecosystems into constantly evolving habitats. Its creatures and users adapt to threats through armour, venom, claws and heightened senses. Survival is sacred to its followers.

Gameplay theme: Mutation, adaptation and evolving threats.

White · Light · Purity · Resonance

The Prism Shard

White stabilises reality, heals corruption and suppresses other crystal influences. Revered as protectors, its users also possess the disturbing power to overwrite free will in the name of unity.

Gameplay theme: Protection, purification and suppression of crystal influence.

Black · Entropy · Void · Consumption

The Illusive Shard

The rarest crystal causes matter to decay and time to behave unpredictably. Null users erase energy and consume crystal powers, but nobody truly controls Black. Some believe it once imprisoned the other six aspects.

Gameplay theme: Entropy, consumption and disruption of crystal powers.

The Fracture

In the late 21st century, Heliox Dynamics discovered a mysterious energy frequency beneath the Earth's mantle. Its patterns resembled thought itself. Promising limitless energy, medical breakthroughs and technological progress, the discovery launched the Deep Resonance Initiative.

At its heart was the World Core Array, a planetary-scale drilling and resonance engine beneath the Pacific Ring. Humanity believed it could safely harness the power below the crust. The machine ran for only seven minutes.

The sky cracked like glass. Mountains erupted into colossal crystalline formations, oceans boiled and cities disappeared in waves of coloured light. Gravity bent. Time fractured. Within months, human civilisation had collapsed.

From the shattered Earth emerged the seven crystals. They changed living things, awakened ancient powers and opened gateways into forgotten realms. But their true origins lay far beyond humanity.

The Crown Beyond

The crystals were never native to Earth. They were fragments of an ancient cosmic entity known as the Crown Beyond, divided into seven aspects to prevent its return. The World Core Array had inadvertently awakened them.

The Return of Ancient Powers

The Fracture shattered time itself. Forgotten kingdoms returned, dragons awoke beneath mountains and mythological creatures crossed through the Vanish Realm. Alongside them rose futuristic cities built on crystal technology. Medieval knights faced AI war machines, spirit shamans confronted mechanised armies and ancient dragons soared above neon megacities. History no longer followed a straight line.

King Calamity

In the early years after the Fracture, a dragon was discovered beneath the Himalayas. Unlike any other creature, it absorbed all seven crystal types. Its scales shimmered with every colour and its breath altered reality. Known as King Calamity, it united scattered factions through terror and nearly fulfilled the Crown Convergence: the fusion of all seven powers into a single divine force.

Had it succeeded, the boundary between Earth and the Vanish Realm would have collapsed, restoring the Crown Beyond. A coalition of rival factions barely defeated the dragon, scattering fragments of its crystal heart across the world. Now every faction seeks those fragments, hoping to gain the power to reshape reality.

The Modern World

Crystal storms sweep continents, cities rise and fall, and ancient monsters roam the wastelands. Crystal-powered armies wage endless wars, each faction convinced that it alone should determine humanity's future — whether to save, evolve, rule or destroy it.

Yet deep within the Vanish Realm, something is waking. Something remembers what the crystals once were, and waits for the seven powers to unite again.

The crystals are not merely tools. They are pieces of a sleeping god, and every battle brings its resurrection closer.
